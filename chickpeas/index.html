The range

Two distinct types. Your specification.

From food processing and packing to wholesale distribution, start with the type, size and quality your business needs.

White chickpeas

Kabuli

Recognised for their light colour and rounded shape. Widely used for hummus, prepared foods, canning and retail packing.

The requested size and sizing basis are confirmed in the agreed product specification. Availability varies by origin and crop.

Brown chickpeas

Desi

Typically smaller and darker than Kabuli. Used in traditional dishes and processed into split chickpeas and chickpea flour.

Your trading partner

International purchasing. Direct supply.

Global Trading AB purchases chickpeas in its own name and at its own commercial risk, holds stock in Europe and Asia and sells directly to customers.

We serve commercial buyers, including smaller businesses buying a few containers. Depending on the requirement, we supply from available stock or purchase for onward sale.

Our role is that of a trading company purchasing and reselling goods. Product specifications, packing, documentation and shipment terms are agreed for the specific sale.
