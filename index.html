How we handle an inquiry

Clear requirements. A straight answer.

For chickpeas we act as principal. For other categories we confirm our role, the supplier details and the terms with each offer.

  1. 1

    Share your brief

    Product, specification, volume, destination and the delivery window you are working to.

  2. 2

    Discuss the options

    We review your request and come back on availability, supply options and anything else we need from you.

  3. 3

    Review the offer

    Specification, documentation, price and shipment terms are set out in writing before anything is agreed.
